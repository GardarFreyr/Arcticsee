Welcome to Arctic See

Authentic Adventures. Extraordinary Landscapes.

Iceland is a country shaped by water and weather. Cold, clear rivers run down from the highlands to the sea, and beyond the last farm the land opens into heath, lava and mountain that few visitors ever reach.

Arctic See Angling and Hunting brings you into that landscape, rod or rifle in hand, at an unhurried pace. Time on the river and in the hills is the whole point, and the country around you does the rest.

Why Iceland?

A Country Still Shaped by Its Nature

Few places in Europe feel this open. Glaciers, lava fields and mountain heath cover most of the island, and the people who live here are gathered along its coast. Step inland and the landscape is yours.

Clear, Cold Water

Rivers fed by glaciers and highland springs, running clean from source to sea.

Room to Roam

A vast, uninhabited interior of heath, lava and mountain, with long views in every direction.

Northern Light

Summer evenings that barely darken, and autumn days when the low sun turns the hills to gold.
